Station Auto Overcharging

Drivers at major railway stations are demanding exorbitant flat rates (often 5x the meter) to transport pilgrims to the main Ghats, refusing to use official meters.

shield_lock How to avoid expand_more
Always insist on booking through the prepaid taxi/auto booths located inside the station premises. Alternatively, use the KumbhAarambh official fare estimator to negotiate or refuse overly high demands.

Unauthorized 'VIP' Darshan Guides

Individuals wearing fake official badges are approaching pilgrims offering "expedited VIP access" to main temples in exchange for large upfront cash payments.

shield_lock How to avoid expand_more
There is no "paid VIP line" authorized by the Mela administration. Do not pay individuals for access. Follow official signages and queue systems managed by the police.

volunteer_activism

Fake Ashram Donation Drives

Groups carrying printed receipts are soliciting donations for "Anna Daan" (free food distribution) but are not affiliated with any registered Akhara or Ashram.

shield_lock How to avoid expand_more
Only make donations directly within the premises of recognized Ashrams or through their official online portals. Never give cash to roving collectors on the streets.
